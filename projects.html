Chiara Malia, my jewelry brand

In Italian, chiara means clear and bright; malìa is an old word for a charm, a little spell. The brand started as waves drawn in the margins of my notebooks, the same curl sketched over and over until it felt like the sea. That curl became the Onda wave.

The first collection, Onda e Marea, takes three ideas from the shoreline: Onda, the wave as it curls over; Marea, the line the tide leaves on sand; Mare, sky-blue stones in a blue I call Blu Cielo.
