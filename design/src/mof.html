Turning tailpipe CO₂
into ethanol.

A copper metal–organic framework (Cu-MOF) catalyst that converts captured carbon dioxide into ethanol, a liquid fuel, using electricity.

CO₂from exhaust
→
Cu-MOF catalystcopper sites in a porous framework
→
EthanolC₂H₅OH, a liquid fuel
91%

Faradaic efficiency to ethanol. Of the electrons that go into the reaction, 91% end up making ethanol rather than side products.

Published with mentors from MIT, Stanford and Washington State.
